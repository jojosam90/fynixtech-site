FAQ

What is Fynix Task?

An AI workspace where specialist agents handle business tasks for you, from sourcing and design to websites and store operations, on the web and on your phone.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Open Fynix Task in your browser. The Android app is optional and shows exactly the same workspace.

Which languages are supported?

English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese and Bahasa Melayu, in the app, on the phone and in agent replies.

Can I use my own server?

Yes. Fynix Task runs with Docker on your own machine or cloud server, and your phone finds it on the same Wi-Fi automatically.

What are credits?

Agents use credits as they work. Every plan includes daily credits, and paid plans add a monthly allowance and top-up packs.
